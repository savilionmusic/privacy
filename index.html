Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

"herm" is a private tool run by Savilion Beats. It is used only by Savilion Beats to manage its own Instagram, Threads and other social accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app reads

On the accounts it is connected to, the app reads posts, comments, replies and direct messages so they can be shown in one private dashboard and answered. This includes the public username and text of people who comment on or message those accounts.

How it is used

This information is used only to read and reply to comments and messages on Savilion Beats' own accounts. Nothing is sent or posted without the account owner approving it.

Sharing

The information is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Storage

A short recent history is kept on a private computer owned by Savilion Beats, so the dashboard can show conversations. Older items are dropped as new ones arrive.

Deleting your data

If you connected an account to this app, you can remove its access at any time in Instagram under Settings > Apps and websites, then Remove next to "herm - IG". This stops all further access.

If you commented on or messaged a Savilion Beats account and want anything about you removed from the dashboard, send a direct message to @savilionbeats on Instagram and it will be deleted within 30 days.
